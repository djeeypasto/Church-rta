Phone mics are not calibrated, so the dB reading is a guide, not true SPL. To calibrate, play a tone, read a real SPL meter, and adjust the offset until they match. Best use: compare before and after EQ changes, spot feedback, and find problem frequencies. Point the mic at the room from where people sit.
